SEÇÃO 2: MÉTODO CASCATA PNEUMÁTICO

Golden Cascata G1: A+B+A-B- (Divisão Direta: [A+ B+] [A- B-], Nm=1)

Sequência canônica: A+B+A-B- | Método: Cascata Pneumático

1. Diagrama Pneumático Cascata (Cilindros, Válvulas Principais, Barramentos L1..Ln, Válvulas Memória M1..Nm)

2. Tabela de Movimentos e Linhas de Pressão

Linha de Pressão Movimento Piloto Acionado Sensor de Chegada Habilitado Por
L1 A+ 1.1:14 1S2 E
L1 B+ 2.1:14 2S2 1S2
L2 A- 1.1:12 1S1 2S2
L2 B- 2.1:12 2S1 1S1

3. Explicação Cronológica do Funcionamento (Cascata)

  1. Start valve E is actuated, pressurizing line L1.
  2. Line L1 pressure feeds pilot 14 of valve 1.1.
  3. The valve of cylinder A switches.
  4. cylinder A advances (A+).
  5. Sensor 1S2 becomes active.
  6. Sensor 1S2 passes line L1 pressure to pilot 14 of valve 2.1.
  7. The valve of cylinder B switches.
  8. cylinder B advances (B+).
  9. Sensor 2S2 becomes active.
  10. Last event of previous group trips activation sensor 2S2.
  11. Sensor 2S2 pilots SET (port 14) of memory valve M1 (0.1).
  12. Memory valve M1 sets line L2 live.
  13. Memory valve M1 resets previous line L1.
  14. Line L2 pressure feeds pilot 12 of valve 1.1.
  15. The valve of cylinder A switches.
  16. cylinder A retracts (A-).
  17. Sensor 1S1 becomes active.
  18. Sensor 1S1 passes line L2 pressure to pilot 12 of valve 2.1.
  19. The valve of cylinder B switches.
  20. cylinder B retracts (B-).
  21. Sensor 2S1 becomes active.
  22. Control wraps to line L1, resetting the memory chain.
  23. The cycle for A+B+A-B- is complete.
